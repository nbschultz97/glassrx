GlassRx Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Overview

GlassRx is a medication and supplement tracking app for Even Realities G2 smart glasses. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how your data is handled.

Data Collection

GlassRx stores all data locally on your device. Your medication information, dose logs, and adherence history are saved using the Even Hub local storage API and never leave your device.

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal health information on external servers.

What We Store (Locally Only)

  • Medication names, dosages, and schedules you enter
  • Dose confirmation logs (taken, skipped, missed)
  • Adherence statistics calculated from your logs

What We Do NOT Collect

  • No personal identifying information
  • No location data
  • No usage analytics or telemetry
  • No data is sent to any server
  • No advertising or tracking

Future Features

Future versions of GlassRx may include optional features such as voice input and cloud sync. These features will require explicit permissions and will be clearly disclosed before activation. This privacy policy will be updated accordingly before any such features are released.

Data Deletion

You can delete all GlassRx data at any time by uninstalling the app from your Even G2 glasses. Since all data is stored locally, uninstalling removes everything.

Third-Party Services

GlassRx v1.0 does not use any third-party services or APIs. No data is transmitted to any external servers.

Children's Privacy

GlassRx is not intended for use by children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
